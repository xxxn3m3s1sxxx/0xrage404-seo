There are no 10x developers. That's a myth sold to you by people who want you to work 10x hours for 1x pay. The '10x developer' is just someone who sacrificed their health, relationships, and sleep to ship code faster than someone who actually tests theirs. Speed without quality is just faster bugs.

Hustle culture tells you to work 60 hours for a feature nobody uses. Check Slack at 11 PM. Reply to emails on Sunday. Push code at 3 AM. For what? A bonus you won't get? A promotion that doesn't exist? Your code rots. Your relationships crumble. It's not passion. It's self-destruction.
