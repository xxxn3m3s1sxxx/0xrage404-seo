Burnout is inevitable if you keep this up. You work weekends. You skip lunch. You answer messages during dinner. Your body keeps score even when you don't. One day you'll wake up and realize you can't write a for loop without googling it. That's not tiredness. That's erosion. Burnout doesn't announce itself. It just arrives and unpacks.

Hustle culture tells you to work 60 hours for a feature nobody uses. Check Slack at 11 PM. Reply to emails on Sunday. Push code at 3 AM. For what? A bonus you won't get? A promotion that doesn't exist? Your code rots. Your relationships crumble. It's not passion. It's self-destruction.
