React devs can't write CSS. You reach for Tailwind before you understand the cascade. You use flexbox for everything, even when grid is the right answer. Your components are style-free zones that depend on a framework to look like something other than a default HTML page. You didn't learn CSS. You learned a CSS substitute.

React was supposed to make UI development simple. Now you have hooks dependencies you don't understand, re-renders you can't explain, and a bundle size that grows faster than your feature set. Your app is 2MB just to render a login form.
