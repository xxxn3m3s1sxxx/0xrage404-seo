AI code never gets reviewed. You paste the suggestion, you commit it, you move on. Nobody reads it. Nobody understands it. Nobody can maintain it. Your codebase is now a graveyard of hallucinated functions that may or may not exist. You didn't write code. You just collected it.

Prompt engineering is not engineering. You type sentences into a chatbot and call yourself a developer. You don't review the output. You don't understand the code. You're not building software. You're collecting outputs from a machine that doesn't understand what it's generating.
