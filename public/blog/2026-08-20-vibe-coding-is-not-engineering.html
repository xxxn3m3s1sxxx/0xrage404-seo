Vibe Coding Is Not Engineering

Microservices for a todo app. Event sourcing for a blog. K8s for 3 containers. You built an empire to ship a form. Every abstraction is a tax on future you. The patch: KISS. No, really. KISS. This video works through what actually matters when you adopt Vibe Coding for real work: the part the polished demos skip, what the documentation assumes, and where the hype diverges from daily experience. It is grounded in shipped code rather than curated benchmarks, and it is aimed at engineers who would rather repair their own stack than re-watch another glossy walkthrough.

The practical view is the honest one. Vibe Coding pays off in projects where the constraints are real: you can measure the trade-offs, you control the migration surface, and the team is small enough to actually decide. It costs you where it is cargo-culted onto problems that never had one, where nobody owns the rollout, or where the vendor narrative replaces your own measurements. Knowing which situation you are in is most of the job, and it is a skill no tutorial hands you.
