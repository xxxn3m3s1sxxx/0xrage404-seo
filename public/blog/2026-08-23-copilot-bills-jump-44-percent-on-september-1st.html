On September first, every GitHub Copilot bill gets up to forty four percent worse — and if your team budgeted against the summer numbers, you are already wrong.

Here is what happened. In June, GitHub quietly swapped Copilot's flat subscription for metered billing: AI Credits, charged per token, at each model's API rate. The sticker price never moved. What money buys collapsed. One developer burned twelve hundred of his fifteen hundred monthly credits on day one and projected a one hundred eighty dollar overage bill for June at that pace. Another user disabled every MCP server he owned and still paid twenty nine credits per message — because the context window itself is billed before you type a word. At that rate, seven thousand credits buy about two hundred forty messages. Eight per day. Including lol.

The escape hatch is gone too. Under the old premium request system, exhausting your allowance downgraded you to a cheaper model so work could continue. GitHub removed fallback entirely. Now the agent stops mid-task until somebody pays. And those budgets meant to protect you? Organization level limits are alerts unless an admin manually flips a hard-stop switch. Default behavior: a runaway agent keeps burning while notifications pile up unread. No rollover either. Use it or lose it.

September first is when it lands. Promotional allowances expire, and permanent pools are smaller: Business seats lose roughly thirty seven percent of their credit cushion, Enterprise forty four. Teams that measured August consumption against promo numbers will discover the cliff in their second invoice of autumn. Developers are already posting quotes of eighty dollar months becoming a thousand.

To be fair: inline autocomplete stays unlimited, and light users will barely notice any of this. The bill targets exactly the workflows GitHub marketed hardest — autonomous agents running all day. Metering honest compute is not a pricing bug; it is the invoice for the hype arriving on schedule. Check your burn rate before September checks you.
